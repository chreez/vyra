We've all had that lane which falls apart for no reason. You're farming, trading, building your items and then suddenly the enemy is destroying your guardian with more souls.

The truth is that's not bad luck—that's laning mistakes, and almost everyone makes the same ones over and over.

## Mistake #1: Not Understanding Matchups

Not understanding matchups might be the biggest reason you're losing lanes. Some heroes in Deadlock are naturally much stronger early into the game than others, and players still force disadvantageous fights because they don't know any better.

### Playing Melee Heroes

The worst thing you can do as a melee hero is try to trade with heroes like Holliday at range. When you finally get up close to deal real damage, you have no health left.

**Key strategy:** Preserve your health pool until you find the perfect moment to get close and strike.

![Veil Strategy](/blog/6-laning-mistakes/screenshot-1.jpg)

Valve implemented veils into each lane to give melee heroes a chance. By sitting on the enemy side of the veil, opponents have no choice but to respect that position. If they try to push you, that's a free kill.

### Countering Melee Heroes as Ranged

Punish them as they come out of the veil. Kill troopers and secure souls from the veil. While they can't damage your guardian or buy items from behind the veil, you can lower their health when they emerge.

## Mistake #2: Wrong Itemization

Too many players open up their build browser, find their top daily build and go left to right each game. Some items will seriously make your lane 10x easier.

**Build to counter your enemies each laning phase.**

For example, when you're against two heroes like Kalako and Mina that build spirit damage, a fantastic response is spirit shielding. It provides:
- Out of combat regen
- Barrier when you take spirit damage
- Movement speed bonus

![Itemization Example](/blog/6-laning-mistakes/screenshot-2.jpg)

## Understanding Power Spikes

You don't need to be a genius and learn every hero's power spike. Just knowing what the character does and adapting your playstyle accordingly will be enough to start climbing the ranks.
