## Before You Start
1. Sit down with your guitar
2. Open this page on your phone
3. Set metronome to 60

---

## Drill 1: Four-Step Chord Preparation
**Just start:** Place all right-hand fingers on the strings where you will play your first chord
1. Hold the strings still with your right hand (strings stop vibrating)
2. Place left-hand fingers on the strings without pressing down
3. Press left-hand fingers down into the frets
4. Pluck the strings with the right hand
5. Return right-hand fingers to the strings for the next chord (strings stop vibrating)
6. Only now release the left hand
7. Slowly place left-hand fingers for the next chord without pressing
8. Press left-hand fingers down
9. Pluck the strings with the right hand
10. Repeat this cycle for each chord in the passage
> **Tip:** "Right hand prepares, holds the string. Left hand prepares the position without pressing. You press. You pluck the strings and you release."

---

## Drill 2: Right Hand Always in Front of the Beat
**Just start:** Place your right-hand finger on the string of the first note you will play
1. Pluck the current note with the right hand
2. Immediately move the right-hand finger to rest on the next string
3. Place the left-hand finger on the next fret position
4. Press the left-hand finger down before you pluck
5. Pluck on the beat
6. Immediately prepare the right hand on the next string
7. Repeat through a single-note melody passage
> **Tip:** "My right hand is always in front of the beat. It's always ready to play the note before the time of that note comes."

---

## Drill 3: Small Finger Movements
**Just start:** Rest your right-hand fingertip directly on a string, touching it lightly
1. Hold your fingertip as close to the string as possible without pressing
2. Pluck the string with the smallest motion you can make
3. Return the finger to rest on the string immediately after plucking
4. Notice the distance your finger traveled
5. Repeat the pluck with an even smaller motion
> **Tip:** "The smaller the movement, the more precise it will be. If I'm close to string, I don't have to rush anywhere."

---

## Drill 4: Position Change with Extra Beats
**Just start:** Play the last note before a position change on the fretboard
1. After plucking, return your right-hand fingers to the strings to stop them vibrating
2. Take two to four extra beats of silence at tempo 60
3. Use more beats for bigger position shifts
4. During those extra beats, slowly move the left hand to the new fret position
5. Place left-hand fingers on the new fret positions without pressing
6. Press the left-hand fingers down
7. Pluck the first note of the new position
8. Gradually reduce the number of extra beats as the shift becomes comfortable
> **Tip:** "Whenever you have a position change, you need to take extra time. Maybe two beats, maybe three, maybe four."

---

## Drill 5: Wrist Position Adjustment
**Just start:** Place your left hand in first position on the neck with your thumb behind the neck
1. Play a note using finger 1 as your guide
2. Notice your wrist angle
3. Move to a note where finger 2 becomes the guide
4. Adjust your wrist angle so finger 2 can reach its fret comfortably
5. Move to a note where finger 4 becomes the guide
6. Adjust your wrist slightly so finger 4 has easier reach
7. Go back to finger 1 as guide
8. Readjust the wrist
9. Repeat these guide-finger switches slowly with the metronome
> **Tip:** "Only this little movement of the wrist can help so much. We have to practice it so when we play in tempo our hand is not getting crazy."

---

## Drill 6: Press Before You Stroke
**Just start:** Place your left-hand finger above a fret without pressing down
1. Place right-hand finger on the string, holding it still
2. Lower left-hand finger onto the string at the fret
3. Press the left-hand finger firmly into the fret
4. Confirm the finger is fully pressed
5. Only now pluck the string with the right hand
6. Listen for zero buzz
7. If there is any buzz, repeat from the beginning with more deliberate pressing
8. Move to the next note and repeat
> **Tip:** "Press before you play. You will never have a mistake. You will never have a buzz. It will always be clear."

---

## Drill 7: Left Hand Release Control
**Just start:** Press a chord with your left hand and pluck it with the right hand
1. After plucking, keep the left-hand fingers pressed on the strings
2. Return the right hand to the strings for the next chord preparation
3. Only after the right hand is resting on the strings, release the left hand
4. Do not lift the left-hand fingers high off the fretboard
5. Hover just above the strings
6. From this hovering position, slowly move to the next chord shape
7. Repeat for each chord transition
> **Tip:** "While I'm still pressing left hand, after I come back with right hand, then I release left hand. I am staying there because I want a small movement."

---

## Drill 8: Two-Bar Repetition Block
**Just start:** Place your fingers at the starting position of the first bar
1. Set metronome to 60
2. Apply the four-step chord preparation to each chord or note in the two bars
3. Play through both bars once at tempo using the preparation method
4. Return to the beginning of the two bars
5. Play through again, checking that right hand prepares before left hand each time
6. Repeat the same two bars five times total before moving on
7. Move to the next two bars and repeat
> **Tip:** "I would repeat these two bars five times before I move."

---

## Drill 9: Adjusting Note Duration to Difficulty
**Just start:** Set your metronome to 60
1. On easy passages, play one note per beat at 60
2. On very difficult passages, play one note per half note (every two clicks at 60)
3. At position changes, add two to four extra silent beats between notes
4. Never change the metronome speed
5. Only change how many beats each note gets
> **Tip:** "That 60 is not a quarter note. That 60 is not an eighth note. The 60 is just a beat."
